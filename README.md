# HR Analytics Dashboard (Employee Attrition Analysis)

An interactive **Power BI dashboard** built to analyze employee attrition and understand why employees leave an organization. It helps HR teams identify turnover trends and make better decisions to improve employee retention.

---

# 📖 Overview

The **HR Analytics Dashboard** gives a clear view of employee data such as departments, job roles, salary, age, tenure, and promotions. It helps users understand where attrition is happening, what factors contribute to it, and which employee groups are most affected.

---

# 🛠 Tech Stack

This dashboard was built using:

* 📊 **Power BI Desktop** – Dashboard creation and visualization
* 📂 **Power Query** – Data cleaning and transformation
* 🧠 **DAX** – KPIs and calculated measures
* 🔗 **Data Modeling** – Relationships between tables
* 📁 **File Format** – `.pbix` and `.png`

---

# 📂 Data Source

The dashboard uses an **HR Employee Attrition dataset** containing employee information such as:

* Department
* Job Role
* Age
* Gender
* Marital Status
* Monthly Income
* Salary Hike
* Years at Company
* Years Since Last Promotion
* Overtime
* Attrition Status

---

# 🌟 Dashboard Highlights

## Business Problem

Employee attrition affects productivity, increases hiring costs, and impacts business growth. HR teams need to know:

* Which employees are leaving?
* Which departments have the highest attrition?
* Does salary or overtime affect attrition?
* How do promotions and experience impact employee retention?

---

## Goal of the Dashboard

The dashboard helps HR teams:

* Monitor overall attrition.
* Identify employees at high risk of leaving.
* Find the main reasons behind attrition.
* Support better workforce planning and retention strategies.

---

## Dashboard Walkthrough

* Total Employees

* Average Salary

* Attrition by Age Group
* Gender
* Marital Status
* Department
* Job Role

---

### 📄 Page 2 – Attrition Drivers & Insights

Focuses on the factors affecting employee attrition, including:

* Salary Slab
* Overtime
* Job Level
* Years at Company
* Years Since Last Promotion

Simple business insights are also included to highlight the key findings from each visual.

---

# 📊 Business Impact

* Helps HR identify departments with high employee turnover.
* Supports better hiring and retention decisions.
* Highlights employees who may be at risk of leaving.
* Shows how salary, overtime, and career growth affect attrition.
* Makes HR reporting faster and easier.
